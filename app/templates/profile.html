How the score works

Score = earned points ÷ configured points × 100. Keywords receive proportional credit; each other configured category receives full credit for an exact match, or zero otherwise. Missing source values earn zero and are flagged. Repeated keywords cannot add points.

For example, 2 of 4 keywords and a matching NAICS earn 25 + 30 = 55 of 80 configured points: 69/100. This is relevance, not a win probability or a bid recommendation.
